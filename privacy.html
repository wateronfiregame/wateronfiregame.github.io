Privacy Policy

Water On Fire, by SoDazzle · Effective 13 November 2026

This policy explains what the Android game Water On Fire collects, why, and what you can do about it. In short: the game has no account, no ads and no in-app purchases, never asks for your name or email, and does not use your device's advertising ID. It sends anonymous crash reports and game statistics to Google Firebase so we can fix bugs and balance the levels.

Who is responsible

SoDazzle is the name under which Anas Khabali, an individual developer, publishes Water On Fire. Anas Khabali is responsible for this data. Contact: sodazzle.it@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings and a play log are stored on your phone only. The play log records how your games went (levels, scores, time played), your device's maker and model, Android version, screen size and language, and a random tester code of 8 characters created by the game. None of it leaves your phone unless you share it yourself (below). Uninstalling the game deletes it.

What you can choose to share: feedback

Test versions have a Send feedback button in the pause menu. It opens your phone's share menu with the play log attached. You pick the app (email, messaging...) and the recipient; nothing is sent if you close the menu. What we receive this way is used only to improve the game.

What the game sends automatically

Data Why Service Kept
Crash reports: what the game was doing when it crashed, app version, device model, Android version, time, and a random installation identifier created by Firebase. Find and fix crashes. Firebase Crashlytics (Google) 90 days
Game statistics: levels started, won, lost or left, progress, time played, hearts, boosters and gems earned or spent; plus app version, device model, Android version, approximate country or region, and a random app instance identifier created by Firebase. See which levels are too hard or too easy and improve the game. Firebase Analytics (Google) 14 months

These identifiers are random and not linked to your name, email, Google account or advertising ID. We do not use this data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not combine it with other data. Ad personalization and Google signals are turned off.

Legal basis

We process crash reports and game statistics on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and improving it (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Feedback you share is processed because you chose to send it.

Google as a processor

Firebase is provided by Google, which processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms. Google may process it outside the European Union, including in the United States, with the safeguards those terms provide. See also Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Children

Water On Fire is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has sent us feedback, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to the contact above. For feedback you sent us, include your tester code (shown in the play log) so we can find it. Crash reports and game statistics carry only random identifiers that we cannot link to you, so we cannot find them from your name or email; they are deleted automatically at the end of the periods above. You can stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the game. You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

Changes

If the game starts collecting something new, we will update this page and its date before that version is released.
