Privacy Policy

Water On Fire, by SoDazzle · Effective [DATE OF THE FIRST RELEASE WITH ADS]

This policy explains what the Android game Water On Fire collects, why, and what you can do about it. In short: the game has no account and no in-app purchases, and never asks for your name or email. It sends crash reports and game statistics to Google Firebase so we can fix bugs and balance the levels. It can show rewarded ads from Google AdMob, but only when you choose to watch one. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before using your data for ads or game statistics, and you can change your answer at any time.

Who is responsible

SoDazzle is the name under which Anas Khabali, an individual developer, publishes Water On Fire. Anas Khabali is responsible for this data. Contact: sodazzle.it@gmail.com.

Who the game is for

Water On Fire is meant for players aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us feedback, contact us and we will delete it.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings and a play log are stored on your phone only. The play log records how your games went (levels, scores, time played), your device's maker and model, Android version, screen size and language, and a random tester code of 8 characters created by the game. None of it leaves your phone unless you share it yourself (below). Uninstalling the game deletes it.

What you can choose to share: feedback

Test versions have a Send feedback button in the pause menu. It opens your phone's share menu with the play log attached. You pick the app (email, messaging...) and the recipient; nothing is sent if you close the menu. What we receive this way is used only to improve the game.

Your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent message the first time you play. It asks whether your data may be used for ads (stored on and read from your device, and used to choose and measure ads) and, through the same answer, whether game statistics may store an identifier on your device. Until you answer, nothing is used for those purposes.

You can change your answer at any time: open the pause menu and tap Privacy options. The new answer applies straight away to ads and game statistics. Elsewhere, Google's message is not shown and the game works as described below without asking.

What the game sends automatically

Data Why Service Kept
Crash reports: what the game was doing when it crashed, app version, build type, device model, Android version, time, and a random installation identifier created by Firebase. Find and fix crashes. Firebase Crashlytics (Google) 90 days
Game statistics: levels started, won, lost or left, progress, time played, hearts, boosters, score modifiers, gems earned or spent, rewarded ads watched or closed; plus app version, device model, Android version, approximate country or region, and a random app instance identifier created by Firebase. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, game statistics follow your consent: if you refuse, or before you answer, Firebase Analytics does not store its identifier on your device (Google's consent mode may still send limited signals without that identifier). Outside these regions, game statistics are on by default. Crash reports are always sent, so that a crash can be fixed.

These identifiers are random. We do not link them to your name, email or Google account, do not sell this data, and do not combine it with other data. Ad personalisation signals and Google signals are turned off in our analytics.

Rewarded ads

Some moments in the game (doubling a level's gems, continuing after a game over, getting a booster) offer to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward. Watching is always your choice; you can also play without it or use gems.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, Google and its ad partners may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), information about your device and the app, and how you interact with the ad. With your consent where it is required, ads may be personalised; without it, Google shows limited, non-personalised ads. The ad partners are listed in the consent message (Manage options, then the list of partners).

For ads, Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can also limit ads on your phone itself: in Android's settings, Google, then Ads, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, for every app.

Legal basis

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads and game statistics rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in Privacy options; withdrawing does not affect what was done before. Crash reports, and game statistics outside those regions, rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and improving it (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Feedback you share is processed because you chose to send it.

Google as a processor

For crash reports and game statistics, Firebase is provided by Google, which processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms. Google may process data outside the European Union, including in the United States, with the safeguards those terms provide. See also Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to the contact above. For feedback you sent us, include your tester code (shown in the play log) so we can find it. Crash reports, game statistics and ad data carry only random identifiers that we cannot link to you, so we cannot find them from your name or email; our copies are deleted automatically at the end of the periods above. You can change your consent at any time in Privacy options, reset your advertising ID in Android's settings, and stop all collection by uninstalling the game. You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

Changes

If the game starts collecting something new, we will update this page and its date before that version is released.
